Sconti Otomasyon

Sconti Otomasyon is the private publishing tool of the YouTube channel Sconti Offerte IT.

The channel presents the current weekly offers of Italian supermarkets and discount stores. This tool prepares the videos on the channel owner's own computer and uploads them to the channel with the YouTube Data API. It also reads the channel's own statistics (YouTube Analytics and Reporting API) to improve future videos.

The tool is used only by the channel owner for this one channel. It is not offered to other users.
